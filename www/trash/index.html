Items to be TRASHED

  • Regular household waste
  • Any painted or stained wood
  • Grass clippings & mulch
  • Dried out latex and oil paints
  • Leaves (from mid-December to mid-October)

Items to be RECYCLED (Blue Cans)

  • Newspapers, magazines, telephone books, corrugated boxes, cardboard cereal boxes
  • Plastics (soda bottles, household detergents, water/milk containers, etc.)
  • Glass bottles and jars (all colors)
  • Tin, aluminum, & bi-metallic cans (rinsed, food removed, caps removed)
Strict Recycling Prohibitions — Do NOT Place in Blue Cans:
NO PLASTIC BAGS (Any size or color) NO SHREDDED PAPER NO PIZZA BOXES (Oily contaminants)

* Recyclable items must be placed in a blue recycling can. No other items will be collected from blue cans.

Note the Following Regulations

  • Separation Required: Trash mixed with recyclables will not be collected until separated. Recycling cans being used for non-recyclables will not be collected until the next regular day.
  • Blue Recycling Cans: Recyclable materials must be placed in a blue can. Cans are available through the Borough.
  • 50 lb Weight Limit: Waste containers and loose waste should not exceed the 50 pound weight limit.
  • Grass Clippings: Homeowners should recycle grass clippings around trees, plants, and bushes for mulch, leave on lawn, or dispose in regular trash.
  • Storm Drain Regulations: Do not rake leaves onto or within 10 ft of a storm drain, and do not blow grass clippings into the street (violates Federal Storm Water regulations subject to fines).
  • Private Contractors: Public Works will not collect trash generated from private contractors or from outside of town.
  • Prohibited Landfill Items: Roof shingles, dirt, and hazardous waste are not accepted by the landfill or incinerator. The resident or contractor must dispose of these properly.
  • Holiday Interruptions: Collections may be interrupted by observed holidays.

Household Hazardous Waste

Hazardous waste is collected by Gloucester County Improvement Authority (GCIA). Call the Office of Recycling at 856-478-6045 or visit www.gcianj.com for free event dates.

Accepted County Disposal Items:
Oil-Based Paint / Stains Paint Thinners Epoxy & Resins Pesticides & Weed Killers Pool Chemicals Antifreeze & Motor Oil Gas / Diesel Fuel Propane Tanks Bleach & Oven Cleaner Fluorescent Bulbs Batteries

Clothing & Textile Program

Place gently used clothing into plastic bags inside the DPW weatherproof bag at the curb on the 2nd Monday of the month. DPW delivers a replacement bag upon pickup.

Permanent Donation Drop Boxes:
  • Public Works Garage (Crown Point Rd)
  • Parkview Elementary School (Birch Ave)
  • Senior Center (Broadway)

Snow Removal Regulations

  • 3 Inch Rule: Plowing commences when 3+ inches of snow have accumulated.
  • Driveway Parking: Park in driveways and refrain from parking in alleys so plows can clear the area.
  • Driveway Aprons: Refrain from clearing driveway aprons until Public Works clears the street.
  • Street Prohibition: Do not blow or shovel snow into the street.

Water Conservation & Emergencies

Water early morning or late evening. Odd house numbers water on odd calendar days; even house numbers water on even days.

Water & Sewer Emergency Contacts:
  • Mon–Fri (7:00 AM – 3:00 PM): Call DPW 856-456-7785 or Borough Hall 856-456-0030
  • After 3:00 PM & Weekends: Call Emergency Line 856-456-0028
