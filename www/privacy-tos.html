Privacy Policy

This privacy statement explains in clear, plain language how user data is utilized on the Welcome to Westville Map Project. We value your privacy and process data solely as necessary to provide features of this website and map.

No Personal Tracking or Collection

This website is a passive, public informational resource. We do not collect, store, track, profile, or sell any personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not set any commercial, tracking, or profiling cookies.
  • We do not integrate third-party web analytics scripts or trackers.
  • We do not require user accounts, emails, or personal login information.

How Geolocation Works (Strictly On-Device)

The interactive map includes a "My Location" button and a "Share my location" feature. These features require your browser's Geolocation API to function:

  • Your exact coordinates are calculated entirely within your web browser on your own device.
  • We do not transmit, save, or log your location coordinates on our backend servers.
  • When you use the "Share my location" button, a custom shareable link is generated locally and copied to your clipboard. This link contains coordinates solely as URL parameters, so anyone you share the link with can see that location, but no location history is recorded by us.

Offline & Local Device Caching

When you visit the site, your browser temporarily saves standard visual elements (like styles, buttons, and icons) on your phone or computer. This helps pages open faster and allows the website to stay responsive even if your internet connection is weak. No personal information, search queries, or location history are ever saved in this local storage.

Third-Party Services

To deliver map graphics, fonts, scripts, and secure connections, your browser connects directly to external infrastructure providers. These services receive standard technical request details (such as your IP address and web browser type) required to send those files to your device:

  • OpenFreeMap (Map vector tiles) — Terms & Info
  • NJGIN / State of NJ OGIS (High-resolution aerial orthophotography) — State Privacy Notice ↗
  • Esri / ArcGIS (Satellite aerial imagery) — Privacy Statement ↗

System Requirements & Browser Support

This community map portal is built using standard web technologies to deliver a fast, secure, and interactive experience for all Westville residents without requiring any downloads.

Minimum Requirements

To access all features of this site, including the interactive map, your browser and device should support:

  • Graphics Acceleration (WebGL 2): This allows your web browser to use your device's graphics card to display and animate the interactive map smoothly.
  • Modern Web Layouts (Web Components): Helps load standard parts of the page, like the main menu and footer, reliably and quickly.
  • Modern Code Execution: Enables the website's advanced interactive features (like instant searching, live maps, and filter updates) to run quickly without slowing down your device.

Supported Browser Versions

We actively support and optimize for the following browser baselines on desktop and mobile:

  • Apple Safari: Version 15 or later (macOS, iOS, iPadOS)
  • Google Chrome: Version 93 or later (All devices)
  • Mozilla Firefox: Version 65 or later (All devices)
  • Microsoft Edge: Version 93 or later (All devices)

Note on Compatibility: If your device or browser does not support 3D graphics acceleration, you can still fully use our Public Directory to search local establishments, find phone numbers, call businesses, and open directions on Google Maps. Only the interactive map rendering canvas itself will be disabled.
