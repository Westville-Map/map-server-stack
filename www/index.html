How to Use the Map

The map is designed to let you filter exactly what you want to see. When you open the map:

  • ⚙️
    Toggle Layers: Use the menu to turn different categories on and off (like Parking, Recreation, or Satellite view).
  • 📍
    Click for Details: Click on any highlighted feature or pin on the map to see more information, such as facility hours or parking rules.
  • ⚡
    Use Shortcuts: Use the quick links below to jump directly to a pre-filtered view of the town.
