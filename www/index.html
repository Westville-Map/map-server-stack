Interactive Map Guide

Welcome to the Westville Map Portal! This interactive companion helps you explore public amenities, local establishments, parking zones, and park activities in the township. Here is a guide to the key features:

  • 🗺️
    Interactive Map & Layers: Toggle standard street view, recreation parks, public parking, local establishments, or high-resolution satellite imagery using the layer controls inside the map frame.
  • 🎯
    My Location (GPS): Tap the My Location button (crosshair icon below zoom controls) to center and track your real-time position on the map. It works on both mobile and desktop, and only requests location permission when clicked.
  • 📍
    Interactive Establishment Cards: Clicking any card in the directory will center, highlight, and zoom the map onto that establishment. Cards feature category-colored left borders (e.g., gold for auto-related tags) to help you visually identify establishments at a glance.
  • 🧭
    Directions & Website Links: Inside each establishment card, tap the Call button to place a call to the establishment, tap the Directions button to get precise navigation routes on Google Maps, or tap Website to visit their online site directly.
  • 🔍
    Advanced Multi-Filter Search: Type terms into the search bar or use the "All Categories & Tags" multi-select dropdown. Filters instantly narrow down both the public directory cards and map markers in real-time.
  • ⚡
    Quick View Shortcuts: Use the collapsible Quick Views & Shortcuts container to load pre-configured views (e.g. Free Public Parking, Local Establishments, Satellite Layer) in one click. On mobile devices, this container collapses to save screen space.
  • 🔗
    Instant Share: The URL updates dynamically as you search, toggle layers, or select cards. Tap "Share" in the map header to copy a link that restores your exact view, layers, and selections. You can also click the dropdown arrow on the "Share" button and click "Share my location" to generate and copy a specialized link containing your current coordinates (this clears any active card highlight to protect privacy).
  • 🖨️
    Custom Printing & PDFs: Engage the printer icon next to the "Full Page Map" button to configure customized landscape or portrait print setups or generate multi-page PDFs. Printing directory cards is disabled by default to save ink but can be toggled on if desired.
