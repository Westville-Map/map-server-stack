Help

Welcome to the Westville Map Portal! This site helps you explore public amenities, local establishments, parking zones, and recreation areas in the township. Here is a guide to the key features:

  • Interactive Map & Layers

    Toggle standard street view, recreation parks, public parking, local establishments, or high-resolution satellite imagery using the layer controls in the map header.

  • My Location (GPS)

    Tap the My Location button (crosshair icon below zoom controls) to center and track your real-time position on the map. It works on both mobile and desktop, and only requests location permission when clicked.

  • Interactive Establishment Cards

    Clicking any card in the directory will center, highlight, and zoom the map onto that establishment. Cards feature category-colored left borders (e.g., gold for local establishments, blue for public parking, green for recreation areas) to help you visually identify establishments at a glance.

  • Directions & Website Links

    Inside each directory card, tap the Call button to place a call to the establishment, tap the Directions button to get precise navigation routes on Google Maps, or tap Website to visit their online site directly.

  • Filtering & Sorting Searches

    Type terms into the search bar or use the "All Categories & Tags" dropdown. Filters instantly narrow down both the public directory cards and map markers in real-time. You can also sort the list alphabetically or by tag using the sort selector.

  • Sharing

    Tap "Share" in the map header to copy a link that restores your exact view, layers, and selections. The URL updates dynamically as you search, toggle layers, or select cards, so you'll always be sharing exactly what you're seeing. You can also click the dropdown arrow on the "Share" button and click "Share my location" to generate and copy a specialized link containing your current coordinates (this clears any active card highlight to protect privacy). No other identifying information is shared and we do not keep any records of the locations of our visitors (see Privacy & TOS for more details).

  • Printing & Saving PDFs

    Tap the Print button in the map header to configure customized landscape or portrait print setups or generate multi-page PDFs. Printing directory cards is disabled by default to save ink but can be toggled on if desired.
