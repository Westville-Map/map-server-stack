# Westville-Map

Welcome to the **Westville-Map** organization — the open-source civic technology and digital cartography initiative for the Borough of Westville, New Jersey.

Our mission is to provide residents, visitors, and municipal stakeholders with modern, privacy-respecting, lightweight tools to discover and navigate community resources, local businesses, public spaces, and municipal services.

---

## 🏛️ About the Organization

Established with authorization from the Westville Borough Council, the **Westville-Map** organization hosts public civic software projects designed to promote transparency, local economic vibrancy, and accessible municipal geographic information.

While built specifically for Westville, our software stack is released openly so other small municipalities and community organizations can deploy similar high-performance portals without enterprise subscription fees or vendor lock-in.

---

## 🗺️ Current Projects

### [map-server-stack](https://github.com/Westville-Map/map-server-stack)
Our flagship municipal web portal and interactive WebGL mapping engine:
* **Live Portal**: [westville.app](https://westville.app)
* **Live Interactive Map**: [map.westville.app](https://map.westville.app)
* **Stack**: [MapLibre GL JS](https://maplibre.org/), [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/) vector tiles, [Caddy](https://caddyserver.com/) edge web server with ETag caching, [Node.js/Express](https://expressjs.com/) atomic GeoJSON backend, and client-side PDF directory generation with [pdf-lib](https://pdf-lib.js.org/).
* **License**: [GNU AGPL-3.0](https://github.com/Westville-Map/map-server-stack/blob/main/LICENSE.md)

## 🚀 Potential & Future Projects

The Westville-Map organization is intended as an umbrella for civic tech applications across the Borough:
* **Municipal Data & Spatial Utilities**: Open tools for transforming municipal spreadsheets and public records into clean GeoJSON layers.
* **Community Calendars & Event Mapping**: Interactive spatial scheduling for borough celebrations, parades, and seasonal festivals.
* **Public Works & Service Visualizers**: Visual schedules for trash, recycling, and street maintenance.

---

## 💡 Tech & Design Principles

* **Privacy by Design**: No commercial trackers, no advertising pixels, and no invasive telemetry.
* **Open Source First**: Powered entirely by open-source libraries and open datasets (OpenStreetMap, NJGIN).
* **High Efficiency**: Flat-file storage, edge compression, conditional HTTP caching, and minimal runtime dependencies.
* **Accessible & Responsive**: Fully responsive UI designed for desktop, mobile, and print.
